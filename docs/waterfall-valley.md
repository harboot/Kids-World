# Hidden Waterfall Valley

Open the public area from the area picker. The ten missions can be completed in any order, and the boat, train, and lift are available throughout the visit.

| Mission | How to complete it |
| --- | --- |
| Boat Ride | Drive into the blue beam beneath the waterfall. It appears when you drive the boat. |
| Feed the Deer | Approach any deer and choose **Feed Deer**. You can also feed rabbits and return to feed any animal again. |
| Fishing | Use the spare rod at the fishing pier. Wait for **Catch Fish**, then reel in the fish. Choose **Cancel Fishing** while waiting or walk away from the pier to stop. |
| Gold Hunt | Walk up to three gold nuggets along the hiking trails at different elevations. |
| Campfire | Choose **Light Campfire** at the riverside campsite and let night settle. The campfire stays lit until you choose **Extinguish Campfire**. |
| Waterfall Discovery | Reach both cave entrances behind the waterfall, in either order. |
| Restore Mine Power | Choose **Start Generator** at the underground power station control panel. |
| Crystal Mining | Approach a deposit to equip the pickaxe. Choose **Mine Crystal** three times at each of the three deposits. |
| Mine Train | Drive through the three amber beams along the main rail line. Checkpoints count in either direction. You can dismount and resume driving from where you parked. |
| Flying Fox | Choose **Ride Flying Fox** outside the upper cave and ride the cable to the valley floor. |

Use the displayed interaction prompt with Space on desktop or tap it on a touchscreen. The ranger, camper, fisherman, and miner give short local hints. Choose **Reset** beside **Resume exploring** to return to the spawn point without reloading. A reset or new visit restores daytime and clears in-visit progress. The area picker keeps the completion badge once all ten missions have been earned.
